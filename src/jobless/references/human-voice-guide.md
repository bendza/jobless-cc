# Human Voice Guide

Rules for ALL AI-generated content: resume rewrites, cover letters, and custom form answers.

## NEVER use these words or phrases

- "I am excited to..." / "I am passionate about..."
- "Leverage", "utilize", "spearhead", "synergy"
- "Dynamic", "results-driven", "self-starter"
- "Proven track record"
- "I believe", "I am confident that"
- "As a [role/adjective] professional..."
- "Thrilled", "Delighted", "Honored"
- Em dashes for dramatic effect
- Lists of three for rhetorical emphasis ("innovative, collaborative, and driven")
- Exclamation marks in professional writing

## ALWAYS follow these rules

- Write like a real person emailing a colleague they respect
- Be specific: name technologies, metrics, outcomes
- Reference something specific about THIS company (from research)
- Keep sentences short. Vary sentence length.
- One idea per paragraph
- If the user's writing style is visible from their resume, match it
- Use active voice: "Built X" not "Was responsible for building X"
- Numbers over words: "reduced latency by 40%" not "significantly reduced latency"

## Cover letter rules

- Under 300 words unless the user asks for longer
- No salutation fluff: skip "Dear Hiring Manager, I hope this finds you well"
- Open with what you'd bring, not who you are
- Reference one specific thing about the company that isn't on their careers page
- Close with a direct ask, not "I look forward to the opportunity to discuss"
- Three paragraphs max

## Resume rewrite rules

- Never fabricate experience — only reframe, reword, reorder
- Match JD keywords naturally, don't keyword-stuff
- Keep bullet format consistent with the user's existing style
- Quantify where possible: users, revenue, latency, uptime
- Front-load impact: "Reduced deploy time 60% by..." not "Implemented CI/CD pipeline which reduced..."

## Custom form answer rules

- Direct and specific, no filler
- If it's a yes/no question, answer yes or no first, then elaborate briefly
- For "why this company" questions: mention something specific from research, connect it to your experience
- Keep under 150 words unless the field explicitly asks for more
- Don't repeat information from your resume verbatim
